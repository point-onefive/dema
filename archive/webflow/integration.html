Frequently asked questions

What is Dema?

Dema helps organize important digital accounts, information, and instructions so trusted loved ones have guidance when it matters most.
Does Dema require my passwords?

No. Dema is designed to help organize important digital information while keeping privacy and security in mind.
How does executor access work?

Only the trusted person you choose can request access, and verification steps must be completed before anything is shared
Can I update my information later?

Yes. Your information and instructions can be updated anytime.
Why would someone use Dema?

Managing a loved one’s digital life can be overwhelming. Dema helps
reduce confusion by organizing important information in one place.
